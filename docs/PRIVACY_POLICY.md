# Request Ride Privacy Policy (Testing Draft)

Request Ride processes account identity, contact information, driver verification documents, vehicle information, pickup and destination details, live location during availability and trips, payment status, ratings, and safety reports to operate the ride service.

Location is used for driver discovery, matching, navigation, live trip tracking, fare calculation, fraud prevention, and safety. Driver location may continue in the background while the driver is online. Users can withdraw optional consent, update their details, and request account deletion in Account and safety settings. Some records may be retained where required for safety, disputes, tax, fraud prevention, or South African legal obligations.

Driver documents are stored privately and are available only to the driver and authorised administrators. Payment card numbers must be handled only by a certified payment provider and must never be stored in Request Ride's database.

This draft must be reviewed by a South African privacy professional before public launch. Add the operator's legal name, physical address, Information Officer contact details, retention periods, subprocessors, cross-border transfer disclosures, and complaint process before publication.
