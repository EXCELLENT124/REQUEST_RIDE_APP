# Request Ride Terms of Service (Testing Draft)

Request Ride connects customers with independently registered and approved drivers. Only eligible cars are accepted. Drivers must keep licences, permits, roadworthy certificates and insurance valid and must follow applicable South African transport and road-safety requirements.

Fare estimates use configured base, distance, time, booking and minimum charges. The final fare is calculated after trip completion from recorded trip distance and duration. Cash remains payable directly. Card payments are unavailable until an approved payment provider is connected.

Users must not misuse location, safety, rating, document, or account systems. Emergency tools do not replace police, ambulance, fire or other emergency services; call 112 when anyone is in immediate danger.

This draft is for testing and requires legal review, operator identification, consumer-protection terms, cancellation/refund rules, insurance disclosures and dispute procedures before public launch.
